# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 7, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Necromate: Chess Roguelite** (“the App”) handles information when you use it. The App is developed and published by **Baborama Studios**.

The App is a chess roguelite: you play a necromancer king who fights a gauntlet of chess bosses, grows an army between battles, and spends what each run leaves behind on lasting unlocks.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how data is handled.

## 2. Data Collection and Sharing Summary

Baborama Studios does not collect your data, does not operate a server for the App, does not require an account, and does not sell your data.

Your game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

The App shows ads through **Google AdMob**: optional rewarded ads you choose to watch, and an occasional full-screen ad after a lost run. When ads are requested or shown, Google's advertising software in the App collects certain device data, as described in section 6.

## 3. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following locally on your device:

* **Game Progress:** Your run in progress (so you can leave and resume it), bones, unlocked armies, abilities and themes, achievements, run history and statistics, and a skill rating used to adjust the bosses' difficulty to your play
* **Settings:** Sound, board theme, and similar preferences
* **Purchases:** Whether you have bought the one-time supporter upgrade
* **Ad Frequency:** How many runs have ended since the last full-screen ad and when it played, so ads are not shown too often

This data is used only to run the game. It is not sent to Baborama Studios.

## 4. Device Backup

On Android, your device may back up the App's data to your Google account through Android's own backup service, so your progress can be restored if you reinstall the App or move to a new device. This backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by Baborama Studios, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

## 5. How Your Data Is Used

Your game data is used to:

* Save and restore your game, including a run in progress
* Calculate rewards, unlocks, and the bosses' difficulty
* Show your statistics and achievements

We do not use your game data for analytics, profiling, or marketing.

## 6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows two kinds of ads:

* **Optional rewarded ads:** You can watch one to retry a lost battle or to double the bones a run earned. These only play when you choose to watch one.
* **Occasional full-screen ads:** After some lost runs, a full-screen ad may play when you leave the results screen. Never on your first run, at most every second run, never after you watched a rewarded ad during that run, and never after a won run.

Supporters (see section 7) see no ads.

To request and show ads, the App uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** provided by Google. Google may collect and process, among other things:

* **Device and advertising identifiers,** such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
* **IP address,** which may be used to estimate your approximate location
* **App interactions,** such as when an ad is requested, shown, or completed
* **Diagnostics and performance information,** such as app launch time and how the app and the advertising software perform

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, to personalize ads. This data is collected by Google, not by Baborama Studios. Google's handling of it is described in:

* [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 7. In-App Purchase

The App offers one optional, one-time purchase that supports the developer: it removes ads, makes retries free, and doubles the bones each run earns. Payment is processed by **Google Play** under your Google account; Baborama Studios does not receive your payment details. The App only receives confirmation that the purchase was made, and stores it on your device (section 3). Google's handling of payment data is described in the [Google Payments Privacy Notice](https://payments.google.com/payments/apis-secure/get_legal_document?ldo=0&ldt=privacynotice).

## 8. Your Ad Choices

* **European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland:** The App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. In these regions, an **Ad privacy choices** button appears in the App under **Settings → Ads**, where you can change your choice at any time. Outside regions where this consent applies, the button is not shown; use your advertising ID settings below instead.
* **Advertising ID:** On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
* **Not watching rewarded ads:** Rewarded ads are optional. You can play the whole game without watching any.

## 9. Permissions

The App uses the following permissions:

* **Internet and Network State:** To load ads
* **Advertising ID and Android ad services:** Used by Google's advertising software as described in section 6
* **Google Play Billing:** To make the optional purchase described in section 7

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it:

* **Reset Progress:** In the App, **Settings → Reset progress** deletes your game progress.
* **Clear App Data:** On supported devices, open your device settings, select the App, and clear its storage or data. This deletes your game progress and settings.
* **Uninstall the App:** Uninstalling removes the App and its locally stored data, subject to your device operating system's normal behavior.
* **Device Backups:** Manage or delete backups of the App's data in your device's or Google account's backup settings (section 4).

Because Baborama Studios does not receive your game data, we cannot access, recover, or delete it on your behalf. For data collected by Google for advertising or payments, see Google's policies linked in sections 6 and 7.

## 11. Account Creation

The App does not require or support user accounts. There is no account data for Baborama Studios to delete.

## 12. Data Sharing

Baborama Studios does not sell, trade, rent, or share your data. Advertising data is collected directly by Google as described in section 6, and payments are handled by Google Play as described in section 7.

## 13. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and Baborama Studios does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App and you have concerns, contact us at the address below.

## 14. Security

Your game data is stored locally on your device. You can protect it by securing your device with a passcode, fingerprint, face unlock, or other security options.
